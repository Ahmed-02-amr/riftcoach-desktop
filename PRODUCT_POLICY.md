# Product Policy

RiftCoach is intentionally scoped as a coaching and review tool.

Allowed design intent:

- Post-game review.
- User-selected learning goals.
- Static matchup notes.
- Build/itemization explanation.
- Local personal trend tracking.
- Gentle non-tactical reminders.

Avoided by design:

- Real-time shot-calling.
- Enemy hidden information inference.
- Enemy ultimate/cooldown timers beyond visible scoreboard-like state.
- Packet inspection.
- Memory reading.
- Input automation.
- Overlays that alter the field of intelligence.

The desktop app should keep most live-mode behavior to recording, progress tracking, and post-game report preparation.
